Aaj ka Panchang FAQs / अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

What is Aaj ka Panchang?

Aaj ka Panchang is the Hindu almanac for today. It lists the tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana and weekday, along with sunrise, sunset, moonrise, moonset, the lunar month, Shaka and Vikram samvat, and the auspicious and inauspicious periods of the day.

Can I see the panchang for any other date?

Yes. Use the date box to pick any day, month and year, or use the Previous day and Next day buttons. The Today button brings you back to the current date.

Why do some timings go past 24:00?

A tithi, nakshatra or karana that ends after midnight is shown with hours beyond 24, for example 26:00 means 2:00 AM the next morning. The panchang day runs from sunrise to the next sunrise.

Is the panchang the same for every city?

No. Sunrise, sunset, moonrise and every muhurat that depends on them change from city to city. Select your city above to get timings for your location.

What is Rahu Kaal and Abhijit Muhurat?

Rahu Kaal is a daily period of about 90 minutes that is avoided for starting new work. Abhijit Muhurat is the auspicious middle muhurta of the day, usually around midday. Both are shown in the daily panchang above.
