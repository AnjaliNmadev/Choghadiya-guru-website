Next Namkaran muhurat / अगला नामकरण मुहूर्त: Fri, 9 Oct, 9:19 PM → Sat, 10 Oct, 6:01 AM · Hasta (हस्त)

नामकरण संस्कार सोलह संस्कारों में से एक है। सामान्यतः यह जन्म के 11वें दिन, सूतक समाप्त होने के बाद किया जाता है, पर परंपराएँ अलग-अलग हैं। शुभ वार: सोम, बुध, गुरु और शुक्र।

Namkaran is the Hindu naming ceremony, one of the sixteen Sanskars. The child's name stays for life, so families like to hold the ceremony at a favourable time. The tables below list nakshatra windows suitable for Namkaran in 2026.

When to hold the ceremony / नामकरण कब करें

The ceremony is usually held after the Sutak period following birth, most commonly around the 11th day, though customs vary. Pick the first suitable window after that, then confirm the exact hour with your pandit.

Good days, tithis and nakshatras / शुभ वार, तिथि और नक्षत्र

Weekdays: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday. Nakshatras: Ashwini, Rohini, Mrigashirsha, Pushya, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Swati, Anuradha, Shravana, Shatabhisha, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada and Revati are widely recommended. Tithis: Rikta tithis (Chaturthi, Navami, Chaturdashi) are generally avoided. For the hour itself, prefer Amrit, Shubh or Labh Choghadiya and stay clear of Rahu Kaal. See the Choghadiya and Rahu Kaal pages.

Namkaran Muhurat FAQs / अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

When should Namkaran Sanskar be done? / नामकरण संस्कार कब करना चाहिए?

Traditionally on the 11th day after birth, once the Sutak (birth-related impurity) period ends. Many families do it on the 10th, 12th or 13th day, or later at a convenient time. Ask your family pandit which custom you follow.

Which days of the week are good for naming a baby? / नामकरण के लिए कौन से वार शुभ हैं?

Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday are commonly preferred. The tables mark these with a tick, and the weekday is worked out from the actual calendar date.

Which nakshatras are considered good for Namkaran? / नामकरण के लिए कौन से नक्षत्र शुभ माने जाते हैं?

Commonly listed ones are Ashwini, Rohini, Mrigashirsha, Pushya, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Swati, Anuradha, Shravana, Shatabhisha, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada and Revati. Lists vary slightly between traditions.

Which tithis should be avoided? / किन तिथियों से बचना चाहिए?

Rikta tithis (Chaturthi, Navami and Chaturdashi) are generally avoided for auspicious ceremonies. Some sources list them differently, so confirm with your pandit.

Is a muhurat compulsory for naming? / क्या नामकरण में मुहूर्त अनिवार्य है?

Not strictly. Many families simply choose a good day within the window after Sutak ends. A muhurat is a tradition-based preference, not a legal or medical requirement.

How is the baby's first letter chosen? / बच्चे का पहला अक्षर कैसे तय होता है?

Traditionally from the baby's birth nakshatra and charan, which gives a set of starting syllables. A pandit or a kundali report can tell you the right letters for your baby.
