Hindu Calendar 2035 / हिन्दू कैलेंडर 2035

हिन्दू कैलेंडर चन्द्र-सौर पद्धति पर चलता है। महीने की गणना चन्द्रमा की कलाओं से और वर्ष की गणना सूर्य की गति से होती है, इसलिए त्योहारों की अंग्रेज़ी तारीखें हर साल बदलती हैं। नीचे 2035 के सभी प्रमुख त्योहार, व्रत और तिथि वाले दिन महीने के क्रम में दिए गए हैं।

The Hindu calendar is lunisolar: months follow the Moon's phases and the year is kept in step with the Sun. That is why festival dates move on the Gregorian calendar from year to year. This page lists the main festivals, vrats and tithi days of 2035 month by month, worked out for Delhi (IST). For city-wise tithi timings, see the Panchang Calendar.

Hindu months and the Gregorian calendar / हिन्दू माह

A lunar month runs from one new moon to the next and has two pakshas, Shukla (waxing) and Krishna (waning). The twelve months and the approximate Gregorian period they cover are listed below.

Adhik Maas in 2035 / अधिक मास

2035 में कोई अधिक मास नहीं है, इसलिए सभी त्योहार अपने सामान्य चन्द्र मास में आते हैं। अगला अधिक मास 2037 में ज्येष्ठ मास में पड़ेगा।

There is no Adhik Maas in 2035, so every festival falls in its usual lunar month. The next Adhik Maas is Adhik Jyeshtha in 2037.

How to use this calendar

Dates are given for Delhi. When a tithi runs across two sunrises, some festivals are observed on one day by Smarta families and on the next by Vaishnava traditions, so you may see two consecutive dates (for example Janmashtami). Where a tithi starts late in the evening, a city far from Delhi can see the festival a day earlier or later. Check the exact tithi timing for your city on the Panchang Calendar or the vrat pages: Ekadashi, Pradosh, Amavasya, Purnima and Sankashti Chaturthi.

Hindu Calendar FAQs

What is the Hindu calendar?

It is a lunisolar calendar in which months follow the lunar phases and the year follows the Sun. Extra (Adhik) months are added about every three years so that festivals stay in their seasons.

Why do Hindu festival dates change every year?

Festivals are fixed by tithi (lunar day), not by the Gregorian date. A lunar year is about 11 days shorter than a solar year, so the same tithi lands on a different English date each year.

What is Adhik Maas?

Adhik Maas is an extra lunar month added roughly every 32 months to keep the lunar and solar years aligned. There is no Adhik Maas in 2035. The next one is Adhik Jyeshtha in 2037.

Why can the date differ from my local calendar or pandit?

Dates here are for Delhi. A tithi that spans two days, a different sunrise time at your city or a different regional tradition (Smarta, Vaishnava, Amanta, Purnimanta) can move the festival by a day. Confirm big festivals with your family pandit.
